Veloce

Design system for the Veloce car-rental app. Renters ask, owners approve who drives. Load the CSS variables and vl- component classes from this file.

Components

BottomNav

BottomNav is the app's main navigation: a floating white pill with icon-over-label items, pinned above the screen bottom.

  • surface fill, 4px inset, shadow-float. Items are 50px tall, radius 26, 22px icons stroked 2.2, labels 11px weight 700.
  • Active item: brand-wash fill, brand content, aria-current="true". Inactive: body.
  • Three to five items. The set changes with the viewer's role (renter / owner).
  • Consumer provides: items (label, icon path), the current key, onSelect.

Button

Button is the pill-shaped action for every tap that does something. Use one primary (brand fill, 56px) per screen; pair it with vl-btn--outline (brand border, 54px) for the alternative.

  • Always a full pill: radius-pill. Never square corners, never a text-only button for a main action.
  • Width is 100% of its container; place two compact buttons (44px) side by side in a flex row for approve / decline pairs.
  • vl-btn--neutral (border line) is for third-party sign-in. vl-btn--danger (border and label danger-action) is only for removal; confirm in a sheet.
  • A disabled button is disabled fill with on-brand label. Say why it is disabled in nearby copy.
  • Focus shows a solid 3px brand ring offset 2px. Do not remove it.
  • Consumer provides: the label (a verb, sentence case), onClick, disabled.

Chip

Automatic5 seatsSedan RAD 123 A Available

Chip is a small fact tag: transmission, seats, body type, or a number plate.

  • Fill is ground on white cards, text ink, label style, radius-sm. Plates switch to mono-tag (JetBrains Mono).
  • The floating variant (surface fill, brand-deep text) sits over a photo, top-left.
  • Chips state facts and are not tappable; use a segmented control or button for choices.
  • Consumer provides: the text. Show a plate exactly as registered, upper case.

Notice

Your insurance expires in 5 days. Renew it to keep this car published.
That password does not match this email.

Notice is an inline message for something the person should read before continuing.

  • Caution: caution-wash fill, caution-body text, 13px, radius-md. Danger: danger-wash fill, danger text, 14px weight 600, role="alert".
  • Put it directly above the control it concerns. One notice per area.
  • Write the consequence and the fix in plain words; no exclamation marks.
  • Consumer provides: the message string and an optional leading icon.

SegmentedControl

SegmentedControl switches between two or three views of the same list, such as Explore and Saved.

  • A white radius-pill track with 4px inset; each segment is 48px, 15px weight 700. Selected = brand fill, on-brand text; others ink on transparent.
  • Use role="tablist" / role="tab" with aria-selected.
  • Not for primary navigation (use BottomNav) and not above four options.
  • Consumer provides: labels, the selected key, onChange.

StatTile shows one money figure with a swatch-keyed label, used in the owner's finance summary.

  • White radius-xl tile, 14px padding; legend is a 10px radius-xs-ish swatch plus a muted label; value is 19px weight 800 in ink.
  • The swatch colour keys the figure to its chart segment. The word always carries the meaning.
  • Lay tiles two to a row with a 12px gap.
  • Consumer provides: label, swatch token, formatted value.

StatusBadge

○ Pending approval ! Approved · payment required ✓ Confirmed ● Active rental ↺ Return inspection ✔ Completed ✕ Cancelled ⏱ Expired ✕ Declined ! Claim pending

StatusBadge states where a booking is in its life: request, approval, payment, rental, return, close.

  • Each state is a word plus a leading glyph (○ ! ✓ ● ↺ ✔ ✕ ⏱), so state never rests on colour alone. Keep both.
  • Map: Pending = caution-wash/caution-ink; Approved = brand-wash/brand-deep; Confirmed and Active = brand-tint/brand-deep; Return inspection = review-wash/review-ink; Completed, Cancelled, Expired = sand/ink-soft; Declined and Claim pending = danger-wash/danger.
  • Wording changes with the viewer: an owner sees "Approved · awaiting payment", a renter "Approved · payment required".
  • Consumer provides: the state key and the label.

TextField

TextField is the single-line input: a muted caption label above a 52px, radius-md, surface field with a 1.5px line border.

  • Keep text at 16px (body-lg) so mobile browsers do not zoom.
  • The line border is 1.5:1 on white, below the 3:1 non-text floor. It is kept as the source draws it; the 3px brand focus ring carries focus.
  • Errors are a sentence under the field in danger, caption weight 700, with role="alert"; set aria-invalid to switch the border to danger. Say what to enter, not what went wrong.
  • Consumer provides: label text, value, placeholder, error string.

VehicleCard is the browse unit for a car: photo, name and daily price, a meta line, then rating and neighbourhood.

  • White surface on ground, radius-card, flat (no shadow), 14px gap between stacked cards.
  • Photo is 190px tall. Until a real photo exists, use the 135° stripe placeholder (hsl(h 8% 88%) / hsl(h 8% 84%), 14px bands) with a mono-cap caption bottom-left.
  • Price is card-title weight in brand, with /day in muted 12px. Money is written RWF 35,000.
  • The heart is a 40px white round button, top-right; label it "Save" / "Remove from saved".
  • Consumer provides: photo or hue, title, price, meta, rating, neighbourhood, availability.

Guidelines

Content fundamentals

  • Write short, concrete sentences in sentence case, addressed to "you". The product's own line: "Veloce connects people who need a car in Kigali with neighbours whose cars sit idle."
  • Say what happens and what to do. Errors read "Enter the 16-digit number on your National ID." and "You must be 18 or older to use Veloce." No exclamation marks, no emoji.
  • Buttons are verbs: "Sign in", "Create account", "Remove car". Money is RWF 35,000, units follow in muted: /day.
  • Status is always a word plus a glyph: ○ Pending approval, ✓ Confirmed, ✕ Declined. Wording shifts by role (owner: "Approved · awaiting payment", renter: "Approved · payment required").
  • Veloce holds payment and the refundable caution fee until pickup is confirmed; say so where money moves.

Visual foundations

  • Ground: every screen is ground (warm cream). Cards, inputs and sheets are surface white, flat, with no border. Quiet wells use paper.
  • One action colour: brand slate blue for primary buttons, prices, links, selected segments and the focus ring; brand-deep for hover and for text on brand-wash / brand-tint. Do not add a second accent.
  • Text: headings and values ink; paragraphs body; secondary muted. Muted is for ground or surface only, never on sand. faint and disabled are never used for text.
  • State colours: caution (caution-wash + caution-ink), danger (danger-wash + danger; danger-action only for the Remove-car outline button), review purple (review-wash + review-ink) for return inspection. Never signal state by colour alone.
  • Type: Figtree at 400–800, headings weight 800 with tight tracking (display -0.03em). Plates and photo captions use JetBrains Mono (plate, mono-tag, mono-cap). Inputs stay at 16px.
  • Shape: everything is round. Buttons, the segmented control and the nav are radius-pill; cards radius-card (22px); fields and notices radius-md (14px); chips radius-sm (12px).
  • Space: 16px screen gutter (space-7), 14px between stacked cards, 24px between sections. Tap targets are at least control-icon (44px); primary buttons are control-primary (56px).
  • Depth: flat by default. Only the floating nav and popovers take shadow-float. Dim behind sheets with scrim.
  • Imagery: vehicle photos fill the card top at 190px. Placeholder is a 135° stripe in a car-specific hue at 8% saturation with a mono-cap caption.
  • Focus: a solid 3px brand ring, 2px offset, on every control. The line border on fields is 1.5:1 and stays as designed; the ring carries focus.
  • Motion: none specified in the source.

Iconography

Line icons, 24px viewBox, drawn at 22px with stroke="currentColor", width 2.2, round caps and joins, no fill. Status glyphs are text characters (○ ! ✓ ● ↺ ✔ ✕ ⏱), not icon files. No icon font or sprite was in the source, so icons are inline SVG paths.
